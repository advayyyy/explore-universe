The orrery

The Solar System, in motion

Each planet orbits at its true relative speed — Mercury races, Neptune crawls. Hover to identify a world, click to open its profile, and use the controls to slow things down or hide the orbits.

Tip: click a planet to read about it

Earth

True relative speed

Orbital periods are set to real ratios. Mercury completes a lap in about 88 days; Neptune takes 165 years.

Not to scale

Sizes and distances are compressed so everything fits on screen. The real gaps are thousands of times larger.

Lit by the Sun

Each planet's day side faces the Sun, so you can see the same terminator line a spacecraft would.
